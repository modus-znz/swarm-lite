# Adopters of Swarm-Lite

`swarm-lite` is utilized in production and development environments for high-throughput subagent swarm orchestration:

- **Modus ZNZ**: High-scale agency swarm infrastructure and Antigravity orchestrator sessions.
- **Antigravity Sessions**: Multi-agent fan-out and token-constrained subtask execution engines.

To add your organization or project to this list, please submit a Pull Request!
